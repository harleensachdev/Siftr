How Siftr works

Three steps between you and a worry-free shop.

Step 01

Set your profile

Tell us what matters — gluten free, vegan, halal, nut allergy and more. Set it once, used on every shop.

Step 02

Point and capture

Open Siftr, aim your camera at any shelf and tap capture. No picking up individual products required.

Step 03

Read the colours

Green means safe. Red means avoid. Amber means check — because "may contain" means different things to different people. Tap any product for the full detail.

GreenFully suitable for your restrictions
RedContains a restricted ingredient
Amber"May contain" warning — your call
GreyCould not identify — scan barcode

Amber warnings — because "may contain" isn't one-size-fits-all

For someone with celiac disease or a severe allergy, a "may contain" warning is a hard stop. For a lifestyle vegan, it's a personal judgement call. Siftr respects that difference and surfaces the right level of caution for your specific profile.
